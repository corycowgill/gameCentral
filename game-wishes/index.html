Our mission is growingGames that help, not just games

Hallucinated Games started as a dad and his two kids building silly browser games with AI. Along the way we learned something: a game that stars you, made from your own ideas, is a special kind of magic. Kids light up when the hero on screen is the character they dreamed up.

So we’re expanding what we do. Alongside the arcade, we’re now building personalized games for kids and families who could really use a fun distraction: kids in the hospital, kids who are homebound or recovering, and families going through something tough who don’t have much to smile about right now.

We can’t fix a hospital stay. But we can make a long afternoon a little shorter, give a kid something to be excited about, and maybe give a worn-out parent a few quiet minutes. That’s the goal: less stress, more fun, and a game that’s theirs.

— Cory, Hallucinated Games

How it worksFrom idea to playable game

  1. Tell us about the game. Fill out the form below with your kid: what the game is about, who the characters are, and what they look like. Wild ideas welcome.
  2. We get in touch. We’ll reply by email or phone to say hello, ask any questions, and confirm the details.
  3. We build it. We design and build the game around your kid’s ideas, and may check in with a preview along the way.
  4. Game on. You get a private link. It plays right in a web browser on a tablet, laptop or computer. No downloads, no accounts, no ads, no purchases.

Good to knowWhat to expect

        

            Does it cost anything?
            
No. These games are a free project. There’s nothing to buy, now or later, and the games have no ads or in-game purchases.

        

        

            How long does it take?
            
Usually a few weeks, depending on the game and how many requests we have. If timing matters, like an upcoming surgery, a long stay or a birthday, tell us in the form and we’ll do our best.

        

        

            Can you take every request?
            
We’re a very small team (one dad, a lot of AI and two kid testers), so we may not be able to say yes to everyone. We give priority to kids in the hospital, kids who are homebound and families with the greatest need. We’ll always reply and let you know either way.

        

        

            What kind of games can you make?
            
Browser games like the ones in our arcade: adventures, platformers, racing, tower defense, puzzles, shooters with goofy blasters, and more. They run in a web browser on most tablets and computers. We keep them kid-friendly and can make them gentle, slow-paced or touch-only if that helps.

        

        

            Who will see the game?
            
The game lives at a private link we send only to you. We won’t post it in the arcade or share your kid’s name or story anywhere unless you tell us that’s OK.

        

        

            How do you handle our information?
            
We use what you send only to reply to you and build the game. We don’t sell or share it, and we don’t need last names, photos, addresses or medical details, so please leave those out. Ask us any time and we’ll delete your request. This page has no cookies, analytics or tracking.

        

        

            Is this a medical or therapy service?
            
No. We make games for fun and distraction. We’re not affiliated with any hospital, and a game is no substitute for care from your child’s medical team.
